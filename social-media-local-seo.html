FAQ

Social media & local SEO questions

What is local SEO?

Local SEO helps your business show up when nearby customers search on Google and Google Maps, for searches like “roof repair near me” or “aircon cleaning Bataan.” It covers your Google Business Profile, reviews, your website and how consistently your business details appear online.

How long does local SEO take to work?

Quick fixes, like completing your Google Business Profile or choosing the right category, can help within weeks. Stronger, lasting rankings usually build over a few months of steady work. Anyone who promises a guaranteed #1 spot overnight isn’t being honest with you.

Which social media platforms do you manage?

Facebook, Instagram and LinkedIn. I set up or refresh your business pages with branded banners, then plan and create posts, graphics and short videos.

Do you work with businesses in the Philippines and the U.S.?

Yes. I work with small businesses in South Florida, Metro Manila and Bataan, and with clients anywhere online. I work U.S. Eastern business hours.

Why combine social media and local SEO?

They feed each other. Social posts send people to your website and Google profile, and strong reviews and search visibility make your social pages more credible. One plan, one consistent brand.

How much does it cost?
