What it needed to do

Most freelancers send a résumé and hope. I wanted a site that does the selling for me: it explains the services, backs them up with real performance results, and turns a visitor into a booked call.

It also had to be something I could update myself in minutes, with no page builder or hosting plan to pay for every month.

  • Explain the services clearly in under a minute
  • Turn visitors into booked calls with no email back-and-forth
  • Build trust with real proof and client reviews

What I built

  • Intro animationA short opening sequence that plays once, with a skip button, and switches off for visitors who prefer less motion.

  • Availability calendarShows open days in U.S. Eastern time. Weekdays link to live booking; weekends go to a quick WhatsApp request.

  • One-tap WhatsApp bookingEvery booking button opens a chat with the message already written, so visitors just press send.

  • Client review systemClients rate and write a review on the site, and it comes to me for approval before it goes live.

  • Pricing page with quote formVisitors pick a package and describe the project; the form turns it into a ready-to-send WhatsApp or email message.

  • Proof galleryScorecards and awards in a click-to-enlarge gallery, so claims are backed by screenshots.

  • Scroll-driven motionPhotos open up as they scroll into view and section headings rise word by word.

  • Mobile-first and accessibleDesigned for phones first, with clear keyboard focus and readable contrast throughout.

  • Automated website check-upA free tool that audits any business website in about 30 seconds, scoring speed, search visibility, ease of use and security, with a live phone screenshot of the homepage.

  • Plain-English fix planTechnical findings are translated into what they cost the business, ranked by impact, with duplicates grouped so owners see what to fix first.

  • Lead capture with full reportThe top 3 issues are free; the rest unlock with a short form. Every request lands in my inbox with the site’s scores and complete issue list attached.

  • Private owner viewA hidden view shows me every issue with its technical name and how to fix it, so I can prepare a client report or quote in minutes.

  • Local SEO foundationSearch titles and descriptions written around web design and local SEO, a Google Business Profile with 26 listed services, and a one-tap review link.

  • Package-tagged portfolioEach project shows which package it was built on, links straight to pricing and opens a full-size preview, so visitors can say “I want one like this.”

  • Swipe-and-tap service tabsServices are grouped into tabs that swipe on phones, with working arrow buttons and the most-requested service shown first.

  • Instant custom invoicesEvery checkout creates a branded invoice on the spot, and a private builder lets me create a custom invoice for any client in seconds, ready to download as a PDF or share as a link.

  • Pay-from-invoice checkoutClients pay a deposit or the full amount straight from the invoice or the cart, by bank transfer or card, with their email and order number already filled in.

  • Secure, zero running costEvery automation runs free, and the check-up is locked so it only works from this site, protecting its daily quota.

A website that sells for you

A site built around one goal: turning visitors into calls, bookings and quote requests.

  • Clear headlines and a call to action on every page, so visitors always know the next step
  • Booking calendar, one-tap WhatsApp and quote forms that send you ready-to-read requests
  • Case study and review sections that answer “can I trust them?” before a visitor asks

What you gain: more inquiries from the same traffic, without paying for more ads.

Get found on Google

Local SEO done properly, so you show up when nearby customers search for what you do.

  • Page titles, search descriptions and headings written around your services and the cities you serve
  • Google Business Profile set up and optimized: categories, full service list, keyword-rich description and photos
  • A one-tap review link and review request messages, because steady reviews are one of the strongest local ranking signals

What you gain: better visibility in Google Search and Maps, and calls from customers who are ready to hire.

Leads on autopilot

A free website check-up on your own site that works as a lead magnet 24/7.

  • Visitors test their site in about 30 seconds and see an overall score and their top issues in plain English
  • The full report unlocks with a short form, so every lead arrives with name, email and their results
  • You get a ready-made reason to follow up, with the exact problems you can solve for them

What you gain: qualified leads that come to you, already aware they need help.

Get paid faster

Professional invoicing and checkout that remove the back-and-forth over payment.

  • Branded invoices created instantly at checkout, or custom invoices for any amount in seconds
  • Clients pay a deposit or the full amount right from the invoice, by bank transfer or card

No monthly bills

A lean setup that keeps your running costs close to zero.

  • Fast, lightweight pages, which helps both your visitors and your Google ranking

What you gain: more of your budget goes into growing the business, not keeping a website online.
